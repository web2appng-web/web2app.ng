Publish to the Play Store

  1. Receive your .apk file from us via WhatsApp and email within 24 hours.
  2. Create a Google Play Developer account at play.google.com/console. One-time $25 fee paid to Google.
  3. Upload the APK and fill in your store listing. Our video walks you through every field.
  4. Roll out. Once all sidebar sections show green, tap rollout.
Note: If Google shows an "unoptimized APK" warning, ignore it. It is a suggestion, not an error.
🍎

Publish to the App Store

  1. Receive your .ipa file from us via WhatsApp and email within 24 hours.
  2. Enrol in the Apple Developer Program at developer.apple.com/programs. $99 per year paid to Apple.
  3. Upload the IPA with Transporter or Xcode. Our video walks you through it.
  4. Submit for review. Apple typically responds in 24 to 48 hours.
Need help? Our team is on WhatsApp throughout the process.

Common questions during publishing

What if the Play Store says my APK is unoptimized?

Ignore the warning. It is a suggestion, not a blocker. You can roll out the release anyway.

Why is my rollout button grayed out?

You have not completed all sidebar sections yet. Fill in store listing, content rating, pricing and distribution, and app release. When every section shows a green check, rollout becomes available.

Do I need to convert my website again when I make changes?

No. Every change on your website appears in the app automatically. The app reads your live website.
